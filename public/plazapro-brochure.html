Core Modules

Everything you need to run & grow

Ten tightly-integrated modules that talk to each other in real time — so your sales, finance and operations teams finally speak the same language.

01

Dynamic Payment Plans

Create custom installment schedules per unit — down payment, monthly, quarterly or milestone-based. Auto-calculates interest, discounts & penalties.

02

Live Availability Checking

Floor-plan driven view of every shop, office & apartment. Instantly see what's available, booked, sold or held — across all your plazas.

03

Ownership Verification

CNIC-backed ownership records with document vault. Verify any unit's rightful owner in one click — ideal for transfers and audits.

04

Lead Tracking & CRM

Full pipeline from inquiry to lock — source capture, agent assignment, follow-up reminders, conversion analytics, and SMS/WhatsApp blasts.

05

Complete ERP Backbone

Accounting, HR, vendor payments, voucher system, profit & loss, trial balance — all pre-wired. No separate Tally or QuickBooks needed.

06

Verifiable Allotment Letters

Auto-generated, QR-coded, tamper-proof allotment letters. Anyone can scan & verify the allotment on your public portal — instantly.

07

Inventory System

Track every unit's status, pricing, size, category & history. Bulk import floor plans, set zone-based pricing, attach brochures & photos.

08

Multi-Project Handling

Run multiple plazas, towers, phases & societies from one login. Switch projects in a click — all data stays scoped, reports roll up.

09

End-to-End Booking

Capture booking details, upload documents, auto-assign payment plan, print receipts & trigger allotment workflow — in under 60 seconds.

10

Due Payment Alerts

Automated SMS, email & WhatsApp reminders to customers before, on & after due date. Dashboard flags defaulters in real time.

11

Role-Based Access

Admin, sales, finance, agent, customer — each sees only what they should. Full audit log of every action, every edit, every login.

12

Reports & Insights

Daily collection, aging analysis, agent leaderboards, project P&L, unit velocity — a BI-grade dashboard that ships on day one.
